Report a problem

Kiko corrected something it shouldn't have

Click Not right on the toast. That word is remembered and never flagged again — no need to report it. If it keeps happening on a word you've already rejected, that's a bug, so tell us.

Kiko isn't detecting anything

Check the language is switched on in the popup — each one is a separate toggle, and a language that's off never runs. Kiko also needs two consecutive wrong-layout words before it speaks, which stops it firing on ordinary English.

Something else

Use Report a problem at the bottom of the Kiko popup. It opens an email with your version and settings already filled in, which saves a round trip. It never includes anything you've typed.

Billing and your subscription

Cancel your subscription

Go to app.lemonsqueezy.com/my-orders, enter the email you paid with, and follow the link they send you. Cancel from there in two clicks. No need to ask us, and no retention call.

Cancelling stops future charges. You keep Kiko until the end of the period you've already paid for.

Get a refund

Within 14 days of paying, email us and we refund in full, no questions asked. After that, see the refund policy — if Kiko is broken for you and we can't fix it, you get your money back.

Find your licence key or receipt

Both are in the confirmation email from Lemon Squeezy, and in your orders. Lost the email? Send us the address you paid with and we'll resend it.

Move Kiko to another computer

Each licence activates on one machine. Install Kiko on the new one and paste the same key — if it says the activation limit is reached, email us and we'll release the old one. No charge for that.

Who you're buying from

Kiko is sold through Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the merchant of record. They handle the payment, the invoice and any sales tax or VAT, and they appear on your card statement — not us. Billing questions can go to either of us.

Your typing never reaches us. Detection runs entirely inside your browser and the words Kiko learns stay on your machine. That means we genuinely cannot look at your text to debug something — so the more you can describe what happened, the faster we'll fix it. See the privacy policy.
