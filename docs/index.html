Questions

The things people ask.

What happens after 30 days?

Kiko asks you to subscribe — $5/month, or $40 for the year if you'd rather pay once and save two months. Nothing is charged during the trial and no card is needed to start — if you don't subscribe, detection simply stops.

How do I cancel?

At app.lemonsqueezy.com/my-orders — enter the email you paid with and cancel in two clicks. You don't need to ask us, and there's no retention call. Full refund within 14 days if you change your mind.

Does my typing get sent anywhere?

No. There is no server. Detection runs inside your browser and the words Kiko learns are stored locally on your machine. Read the privacy policy.

Why does it need access to every site?

Because you might mistype in any text field — your inbox, a search box, an admin panel. There is no fixed list of places to watch. Kiko reads only the field you are typing in, analyses it in memory, and discards it.

Will it flag normal English words?

Rarely — it needs several consecutive suspicious words before it says anything. When it does get one wrong, click "Not right" and it never flags that word again.

Does it work the other way round?

Yes. Typed English while your keyboard was still in Hebrew, Korean or Greek? Kiko reads the gibberish and turns it back into English.

Does it work with apps I use on the desktop?

Kiko works on any website you open in Chrome, and that includes the web version of nearly every desktop app — same account, same messages. Open it in a tab and Kiko is there. Standalone desktop applications run outside the browser, so no browser extension can reach them.

Which browsers?

Chrome today, and Chromium browsers like Edge, Brave and Arc. Firefox and Safari are on the list.
