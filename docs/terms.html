2. Description of Service

Kiko is a Chrome browser extension that detects keyboard layout mismatches between English and Hebrew, Russian, Ukrainian, Korean, Greek, or Arabic, and assists users in correcting their text. Kiko is free to use for the first 30 days after installation. Continued use after that requires a paid subscription.

3. Free trial, subscription and payment

Every new installation includes a 30-day free trial. No payment details are required to start the trial, and nothing is charged during it.

After the trial, continued use requires a subscription: $5 per month for an individual, or $36 per seat per year for teams of five seats or more, invoiced annually to the organisation. Payments are processed by Paddle. Subscriptions renew until cancelled, and you may cancel at any time from your Paddle account or by contacting us.

If you do not subscribe when the trial ends, detection simply stops. Nothing is deleted, and the word lists Kiko has learned remain on your device.
