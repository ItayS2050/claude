🦜 Kiko – Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Our commitment

We want you to be happy with Kiko. If it's not working for you, we'll make it right.

Kiko is currently free to use — there is nothing to refund. The policy below applies to the optional paid plan described in our Terms of Service, if and when it is introduced.

7-day money-back guarantee

If you are not satisfied with Kiko within the first 7 days of your subscription, contact us and we will issue a full refund — no questions asked.

After 7 days

After the initial 7-day period, refunds are evaluated on a case-by-case basis. If you experience a technical issue that we are unable to resolve, we will refund your most recent billing period.

Cancellations

You can cancel your subscription at any time. Cancellation stops future charges immediately. We do not offer prorated refunds for the remaining days of a billing period after the 7-day window.

How to request a refund

Email us at itay@selltech.io with your order details (found in your Paddle receipt). We respond within 1 business day.

Chargebacks

Please contact us before initiating a chargeback. We are always happy to resolve issues directly and quickly.
