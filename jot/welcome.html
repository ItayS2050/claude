Jot is installed.

Three things and you are set up for good.

1 Pin it to the toolbar

Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of Chrome, find Jot, and click the pin. Then it is always one click away — or press Alt + Shift + T from any page.

2 Allow the microphone

Dictation needs permission once. It has to be granted from a normal tab like this one — a browser popup closes the instant the permission prompt appears, which is why this page exists.

Nothing is recorded or stored: Chrome transcribes your speech and Jot keeps only the text.

3 Write the way you talk

Jot reads the timing out of what you type or say, and files the task under it.

  • call mom tomorrow at 5 → tomorrow, 17:00
  • pay rent in 20 minutes → a reminder in 20 minutes
  • standup every monday 9:30 → repeats weekly
  • send invoice friday ! → friday, flagged important
  • book flights #travel → tagged, no date
  • לקנות חלב מחר → Hebrew works too

4 Work and personal, split automatically

Jot reads each task and files it into one of two lanes — the switch at the top of the popup shows one at a time. A blue dot means work, green means personal.

It will get some wrong. Click the dot to move a task, and Jot remembers the words: correct one task mentioning a client's name and every future task with that name follows. Anything it cannot place stays visible under All.

5 Clients and projects, from the sentence

Write finish campaigns for stream and the task is filed under stream. Nothing to set up — the name comes out of the wording. Mention it twice and Jot starts recognising it anywhere, so stream banner sizes lands there too, and it gets its own filter chip.

Wrong one? Click the × on the chip to take it off a task, or open Settings to remove the name for good.

Select text on any page and right-click to send it straight to Jot.
